Standard U.S. Army TC 25-20 / FM 7-0 Four-Question Framework

FOR OFFICIAL OR ORGANIZATIONAL USE

After Action Review (AAR) Report

Ref: Department of the Army Pamphlet / Training Circular TC 25-20 (AAR Process)

FORM ID: DA-AAR-MOD
Standard Army AAR Ground Rules:
  • Professional, candid, collaborative dialogue.
  • Focus on standard operating performance, not personal faults.
  • Identify root causes, not just surface symptoms.
  • Every participant is encouraged to contribute observations.
1

What Was Supposed to Happen? (The Plan / Intent)

Review the commander's intent, mission objective, operational timeline, standard operational procedures (SOP), and training task standards.

2

What Actually Happened? (The Reality)

Establish facts chronologically. What deviations occurred? What unexpected events or friction points altered execution?

3

Why Did It Happen? (Root Cause Analysis)

Examine root causes behind both successes and failures. Analyze leadership, training, communications, equipment, and environmental factors.

4

What Will We Do Next Time? (Actionable Changes)

Identify tangible takeaways. Categorize into what to keep doing vs. what requires remedial action or SOP revision.

What practices worked well and must be preserved?

What friction occurred and how do we fix it?
